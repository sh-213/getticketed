Working at Height — Quick Reference

  • Ladders are for light, short-duration tasks only, and should be secured or footed before use

Sample question from the bonus practice exams

Before each use, what should be checked on a fall-arrest harness and lanyard?

  • Only the buckle, since the webbing rarely fails
  • The webbing, stitching, buckles, and connectors, with a visual check for damage or wear
  • Only the anchor point, not the harness itself
  • Nothing, if it passed its last annual inspection
Reveal the answer
The webbing, stitching, buckles, and connectors, with a visual check for damage or wear.

A harness and lanyard should be visually checked before every use, not just at its periodic formal inspection, since damage can happen between inspections and a fall-arrest system has to work first time.
